    Allmed – Medicine Supermarket

    The Complete Pharmacy

    ALLMED is the first of its kind medicine supermarket to offer a one-stop, hassle-free medicine and hospital equipment retail solution. Sprawling above fifteen hundred square feet of exclusively designed and impeccably maintained inventory space, ALLMED proudly stocks medicines and home care equipment of all kinds, makes and specifications.

    Coupled with this is the state-of-the-art medicine maintenance protocol employed in our pharmacy where temperature and light sensitive medicines are stocked strictly in temperature-conditioned, light-proof special zones. Here we follow a zero compromise attitude so that the very quality benchmarks of certain sensitive medicines are retained till they reach the end-user.

    Besides all these, by way of ultramodern-infrastructure, abundant stock, automated inventory upkeep and emergency supply back up, ALLMED stands formidably tall, prepared to meet medicine supply expectations of any dimension. Needless to say, the whole ALLMED is manned by dedicated medical professionals of proven credentials
